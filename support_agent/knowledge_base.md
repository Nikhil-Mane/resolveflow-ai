# Example Shop Support Policies

## Returns

Products can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The product must be
unused and include its original packaging. Final-sale products cannot be
returned.

## Refunds

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method. Banks usually take
five to seven business days to show the refund after it is approved.

## Shipping

Standard delivery usually takes five to seven business days. Express delivery
usually takes one to two business days. Delivery estimates can change during
public holidays.

## Warranty

Electronic products include a one-year limited warranty for manufacturing
defects. Accidental damage and normal wear are not covered.
